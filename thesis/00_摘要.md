# 摘要

**在日頻配對交易上，決定學習式交易端成敗的是動作空間的形狀，而非學習演算法的
強弱。** 本研究以 S&P 500 成分股 2000 至 2025 年的日頻資料，在單變因設計下檢驗：
當交易端每期可做的決策次數由 0 次（固定門檻）、1 次（每期選一組門檻），放大到
最多 126 次（逐日自由持倉）時，行為與績效如何改變。既有深度強化學習應用於
配對交易的研究各自選定一種動作空間，未有研究在同一條管線內僅變動此一變因；
本研究補上這個消融，其假說、效果量門檻與核對程序皆於回測啟動前提交版本庫。

結果分三段，而三個答案並不一致。其一，在配對來源、對沖口徑、網格格點與訓練
預算完全相同的條件下，三代逐日自由持倉交易端的換手率為門檻選擇式的 **2.1 至
4.1 倍**（BH-FDR 校正後 $p \le 0.0004$，達標 3/3），且 Sharpe 一律不高於門檻
選擇式——**表達能力更強的動作空間反而更差**。其二，將動作空間縮至每期選一組
門檻後，以監督式回歸逐對學習門檻顯著劣於一個擴張窗最佳常數（$p$ = 0.0004），
**增益不來自逐對辨識能力**。其三，門檻選擇式相對固定門檻，於五個配對來源方向
全部為正（年化 +0.239 至 +0.798 個百分點），BH-FDR 校正後 3/5 顯著，增益
最大者為傳統 GICS 來源。

**惟本研究不支持任何可交易的宣稱。** 以閒置現金計息的一致口徑衡量，15 格固定
門檻基準無一跑贏無風險利率，最佳配置的超額亦僅 +0.375 個百分點；
Deflated Sharpe Ratio 41 條策略無一通過 0.95，日均報酬檢定 6/6 不顯著。
**「相對於某個基準較優」與「本身可交易」是兩個不同的主張，本研究只支持前者。**

**關鍵字**：配對交易、動作空間、深度強化學習、門檻選擇、預先登記、消融研究、
統計套利、S&P 500

# Abstract

**In daily-frequency pairs trading, what determines the success of a learning-based
trading module is the shape of its action space, not the sophistication of its
learning algorithm.** Using daily data on S&P 500 constituents from 2000 to 2025,
this study examines how behaviour and performance change as the decisions available
per trading period expand from zero (a fixed threshold), to one (selecting a
threshold pair each period), to at most 126 (freely choosing a position daily).
Existing deep-reinforcement-learning studies each adopt one action space; none
varies only that variable within a single pipeline. This study supplies that
ablation, preregistered before the backtest began.

Three segments yield three inconsistent answers. First, holding pair source, hedging
convention, grid cell, and training budget identical, three generations of daily
free-position modules exhibited turnover **2.1 to 4.1 times** that of threshold
selection (BH-FDR corrected $p \le 0.0004$, 3 of 3), with Sharpe ratios never
exceeding it: **a more expressive action space performed worse**. Second, once
reduced to one threshold choice per period, learning thresholds pair-by-pair
performed significantly worse than a single expanding-window optimal constant
($p$ = 0.0004), so **the gain does not come from pair-level discrimination**. Third,
relative to a fixed threshold, threshold selection was positive across all five pair
sources (+0.239 to +0.798 percentage points annualised), with 3 of 5 significant
after correction, the largest arising from the conventional GICS source.

**No claim of tradability is supported.** On a consistent basis crediting idle cash
with interest, none of the 15 baseline cells outperformed the risk-free rate, and the
best configuration exceeded it by only +0.375 percentage points; no strategy among 41
passed a Deflated Sharpe Ratio of 0.95, and tests of zero mean daily return were
insignificant in all six cases. **"Better than some benchmark" and "tradable in
itself" are distinct claims; only the former is supported.**

**Keywords**: pairs trading, action space, deep reinforcement learning, threshold
selection, preregistration, ablation study, statistical arbitrage, S&P 500

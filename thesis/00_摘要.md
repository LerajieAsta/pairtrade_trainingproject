# 摘要

**在日頻配對交易上，決定學習式交易端成敗的是動作空間的形狀，而非學習演算法的強弱。**
本研究以 S&P 500 成分股 2000 年至 2025 年的日頻資料，在受控的單變因設計下檢驗
此一命題：當交易端每期可做的決策次數由 0 次（固定門檻）、1 次（每期選一組門檻）
放大到最多 126 次（逐日自由決定持倉）時，其行為與績效如何改變。

既有將深度強化學習應用於配對交易的研究，各自選定一種動作空間並報告績效，
未有研究在同一條管線內固定其餘一切、僅變動動作空間。本研究補上這個消融。
形成期採四層架構（特徵、分組、篩選、排序），使不分組、GICS 產業、HDBSCAN、
Agglomerative 與 K-means 五個配對來源之間的差異被限縮於分組層一處；
交易端則利用「可在同一批配對上施行」的性質，使動作空間得以被單獨消融。
消融的假說、效果量門檻、多重比較族與執行核對程序皆於回測啟動前提交版本庫。

實證結果分三段，且三個答案並不一致。其一，在配對來源、對沖口徑、網格格點與
訓練預算完全相同的條件下，三代逐日自由持倉交易端的換手率為門檻選擇式的
**2.1 至 4.1 倍**，三條臂全部同時贏過兩個基準（BH-FDR 校正後 $p \le 0.0004$，
達標 3/3），且 Sharpe 一律不高於門檻選擇式——**表達能力更強的動作空間在日頻上
表現更差**，與「逐日決策可模擬任何固定門檻規則」的直覺相反。其二，將動作空間
縮至每期選一組門檻後，以監督式回歸逐對學習門檻的成效顯著劣於一個擴張窗最佳
常數（配對 $t$ 檢定 $p$ = 0.0004），亦即**增益不來自逐對辨識能力**；其機制屬
資訊科學而非財務推論——訊號本身微弱（Spearman 相關 0.10），而以 argmax 自
多個輸出中選取，會使選取本身引入的雜訊超過訊號。其三，門檻選擇式交易端相對
固定門檻，在五個配對來源上方向全部為正（年化 +0.239 至 +0.798 個百分點），
BH-FDR 校正後 3/5 顯著，且增益最大者為傳統 GICS 來源，其價值與資料驅動分群無關。

**惟本研究不支持任何可交易的宣稱。** 以閒置現金計息的一致口徑衡量，15 格固定
門檻基準無一跑贏無風險利率（最高者僅 +0.009 個百分點）；最佳配置疊加門檻選擇式
後的超額亦僅 +0.375 個百分點，其中尚有 +0.282 個百分點純粹來自少動用資本、
閒置現金多收的利息，不應計為技巧。Deflated Sharpe Ratio 41 條策略無一通過 0.95，
對「日均報酬是否為零」的檢定 6/6 不顯著。**「相對於某個基準較優」與「本身可交易」
是兩個不同的主張，本研究只支持前者。**

本研究的貢獻在於把動作空間設為唯一變因並完整預先登記、分離「動作空間的形狀」
與「逐對辨識能力」兩種增益來源、以三個虛無分布逐一排除最直觀的機械性替代解釋，
以及記錄一組實作與宣告不符的缺陷及其對結論的影響——修正後交易層的相對效果
自 0/5 顯著變為 3/5 顯著。

**關鍵字**：配對交易、動作空間、深度強化學習、門檻選擇、預先登記、消融研究、
統計套利、S&P 500

# Abstract

**In daily-frequency pairs trading, what determines the success of a learning-based
trading module is the shape of its action space, not the sophistication of its
learning algorithm.** Using daily data on S&P 500 constituents from 2000 to 2025,
this study tests this proposition under a controlled single-variable design,
examining how behaviour and performance change as the number of decisions available
per trading period is expanded from zero (a fixed threshold), to one (selecting one
threshold pair per period), to at most 126 (freely choosing a position each day).

Existing studies applying deep reinforcement learning to pairs trading each adopt one
action space and report performance; none holds everything else fixed within a single
pipeline and varies only the action space. This study supplies that ablation. The
formation stage uses a four-layer architecture (features, grouping, screening,
ranking) so that differences among five pair sources — no grouping, GICS sectors,
HDBSCAN, agglomerative clustering, and K-means — are confined to the grouping layer
alone; the trading stage exploits the fact that any trading module can be applied to
the same set of pairs, allowing the action space to be ablated in isolation. The
hypotheses, effect-size thresholds, multiple-comparison family, and execution
verification procedure were all committed to version control before the backtest began.

The empirical results fall into three segments, and the three answers are not
consistent with one another. First, holding pair source, hedging convention, grid
cell, and training budget identical, three generations of daily free-position trading
modules exhibited turnover **2.1 to 4.1 times** that of the threshold-selection
module, with all three arms beating both baselines simultaneously (BH-FDR corrected
$p \le 0.0004$, 3 of 3), and Sharpe ratios never exceeding it. **A more expressive
action space performed worse**, contrary to the intuition that daily decisions can
replicate any fixed-threshold rule. Second, once the action space was reduced to one
threshold choice per period, learning thresholds pair-by-pair via supervised
regression performed significantly worse than a single expanding-window optimal
constant (paired $t$-test, $p$ = 0.0004) — **the gain does not come from
pair-level discrimination**. The mechanism is informational rather than financial:
the underlying signal is weak (Spearman correlation 0.10), and selecting by argmax
across multiple outputs introduces more noise than the signal carries. Third,
relative to a fixed threshold, the threshold-selection module produced positive
effects across all five pair sources (+0.239 to +0.798 percentage points annualised),
with 3 of 5 significant after BH-FDR correction; the largest gain arose from the
conventional GICS source, so its value is unrelated to data-driven clustering.

**This study nonetheless supports no claim of tradability.** Measured on a consistent
basis that credits idle cash with interest, none of the 15 fixed-threshold baseline
cells outperformed the risk-free rate (the best reaching only +0.009 percentage
points); the best configuration with threshold selection layered on exceeded the
risk-free rate by merely +0.375 percentage points, of which +0.282 points derive
purely from deploying less capital and earning interest on the remainder, and should
not be credited as skill. No strategy among 41 passed a Deflated Sharpe Ratio of 0.95,
and tests of whether mean daily return differs from zero were insignificant in all six
cases. **"Better than some benchmark" and "tradable in itself" are distinct claims,
and this study supports only the former.**

The contributions are: treating the action space as the sole experimental variable
under full preregistration; separating the shape of the action space from pair-level
discrimination as two distinct sources of gain; ruling out the most immediate
mechanical alternative explanations against three null distributions; and documenting
a set of discrepancies between implementation and stated method, together with their
effect on the conclusions — after correction, the relative effect at the trading layer
moved from 0 of 5 significant to 3 of 5.

**Keywords**: pairs trading, action space, deep reinforcement learning, threshold
selection, preregistration, ablation study, statistical arbitrage, S&P 500
